`<MentionInput>` keeps editable text and structured references in one controlled value. Sources provide their own data, identity, inserted text, and suggestion presentation.

## Compose with multiple sources

This example combines local member and team sources. Selected references remain editable and are emphasized while their range is valid.

Suggestion objects stay within their source. The controlled value stores only stable IDs and text ranges.
